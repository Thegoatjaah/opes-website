What we collect, and why

Newsletter sign-up

When you sign up through the pop-up on this site, we collect:

  • Your first name — so we can address the emails to you
  • Your email address — so we can send you the newsletter
  • Your consent — a record that you ticked the box, and when

We use this only to send you the newsletter: marketing tactics, teardowns and commentary on lifecycle and email marketing, roughly once a month. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it for anyone else's marketing.

Enquiries

If you contact us or book a call, we process the details you give us in order to reply and, if it goes further, to discuss working together.

Our lawful basis

For the newsletter, our lawful basis is your consent (UK GDPR Article 6(1)(a)). You give it by ticking the box on the sign-up form. You can withdraw it at any time — see Your rights — and withdrawing it does not affect anything we did with your data before you withdrew it.

For the guide account, our lawful basis is performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)) — you asked for the guide and the account is how we give it to you. Marketing to that address afterwards would rest on consent, separately given.

For enquiries, our lawful basis is legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)): responding to someone who has contacted us about our services.

How long we keep it

We keep your newsletter details until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. If you unsubscribe we keep a minimal suppression record — your email address and the fact you opted out — so that we do not accidentally email you again. That record is kept indefinitely, because deleting it would defeat its purpose.

Your guide account stays until you ask us to delete it. Ask and we remove the account, the profile row behind it and your access, and you lose the guide with it.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal data we hold about you, and get a copy
  • Have inaccurate data corrected
  • Have your data erased
  • Restrict or object to how we use it
  • Receive your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw your consent at any time

The fastest way to withdraw consent is the unsubscribe link at the bottom of any email we send — one click, no questions. For anything else, email us at [ hello@yourdomain.co.uk ] and we will respond within one month.

If you are not happy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113. We would rather you came to us first so we can put it right.

Cookies and storage

Nothing that tracks you runs unless you say yes. When you first arrive we ask, in a bar at the bottom of the page, whether we may use the Meta advertising pixel. Decline it, or ignore it, and the pixel never loads — Meta is told nothing about your visit. Nothing on this site needs it in order to work.

If you accept, the pixel lets us see which of our ads brought people here and which of those people went on to get in touch. That data goes to Meta, whose privacy policy covers what they do with it, and the pixel sets its own cookies on your device.

These are kept in your own browser, on your own device:

  • Your cookie choice — so we do not ask again on every page. Records only whether you accepted or declined.
  • A newsletter pop-up flag — so it does not reappear once you have dismissed or joined it. Cleared when you clear your browser data.
  • A sign-in session for the free guide — so you stay signed in between pages and visits. This one is strictly necessary: without it the sign-in could not work at all. Signing out removes it.
  • Which ad you came from — if you arrived by clicking one of our ads, we keep the campaign details so we can tell which ad led to an enquiry. Only stored if you accepted the pixel.

Changed your mind? Clearing this site’s data in your browser resets your choice and we will ask again next time. You can also manage what Meta shows you in your Meta ad settings.
