The guarantee

You don’t pay unless we deliver on our promise

A promise with no number behind it is just a sentence. Here is the number, and how we agree it with you.

Your Performance Threshold

Your Performance Threshold is the single number your work with us is judged against. It is not a figure we hand you. We set it together on the discovery call, starting from where your revenue actually sits today.

That starting point is your baseline. The threshold is the number we commit to taking it to, and we have 21 days from the start of the work to get there.

  1. We agree the number On the call we look at your current figures together and settle on a baseline we both recognise, then on the threshold we are taking it to.
  2. We put it in writing Your baseline, your threshold and the 21 day window go in writing before any work starts. No work begins on a number that has not been agreed.
  3. Miss it and you don’t pay Reach the threshold inside the window and we have done what we said. Fall short of it and you do not pay us for the work.
  • The number is yours, not ours. We do not set a threshold you have not agreed to, and we will tell you on the call if we think a number is not reachable in 21 days rather than sign up to it.
  • The window starts when the work does. Not at the call, and not at the contract. The 21 days run from the day we begin.
  • It is written down first. If it is not in writing before work starts, it is not the threshold.
